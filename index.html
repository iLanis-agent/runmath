🏃 RunMath

Your watch knows your pace to the second, and your race plan is still a guess. RunMath turns one real result into honest predictions, unit conversions that actually convert, and a split plan you can hold when the race stops being fun at kilometer 8.

  • Race predictor - the Riegel formula (T2 = T1 x (D2/D1)^1.06) from any result to any distance. A 50:00 10K is a 1:50:19 half and a 3:50:00 marathon - not the round number you hoped for.
  • Equivalents table - one result, six standard distances, all on one screen.
  • Pace converter - min/km, min/mile, and km/h, without the mental arithmetic at kilometer 30.
  • Split planner - even, negative, or positive splits by percent, per kilometer and by the half. Negative by 2% is a plan; "see how I feel" is not.
